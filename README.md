# Revit Projects

Building information models (BIM) and planning drawings produced in **Autodesk Revit**, with drafting in **AutoCAD** and rendering exports for **Lumion**. Most of this work comes from the University of Peradeniya module **CE2130**.

![Ground floor plan, Uni Project 1](Uni%20Project%201%20CE2130/images/ground-floor-plan.png)

## Skills demonstrated

- Architectural modelling: walls, floors, roofs, stairs, doors and windows
- Producing coordinated plans, elevations and sections (X-X, Y-Y) from one model
- Gridlines, dimensioning, room tagging, scale bars and north arrows (1:100 plans)
- Foundation, column and retaining-wall detail drawings
- Exporting to AutoCAD (DWG/DXF) and to Lumion (DAE) for rendering

## Opening the files

The drawing PDFs and preview images can be viewed directly on GitHub. Open the `.rvt` models in **Revit 2022 or later**, and the `.dwg` files in AutoCAD or the free Autodesk Viewer.
